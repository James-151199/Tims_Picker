What to look for to evaluate Tim's picks

Individual
- Goals that season
- Goals in past 5 games
- What line they play on
- Do they play powerplay
	- 1st or 2nd line

Team Based
- Teams record in the last 10
- Teams ranking in goals for
- Team PowerPlay percentage

- Opposing teams record in last 10
- Opposing teams record in goals against
- Opposing teams PK percentage
